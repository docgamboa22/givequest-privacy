GiveQuest is built on a simple idea: play games, raise money for causes that matter. To honor that, we collect as little personal information as possible — and never sell it.

01Who We Are

GiveQuest ("the App," "we," "our") is developed and operated by [Your Full Legal Name], an independent sole developer based in California, United States. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the GiveQuest mobile application on iOS or Android.

By using GiveQuest, you agree to the practices described below.

02Information We Collect

GiveQuest does not require an account. You don't sign up, log in, or give us your name or email.

Information you provide

  • Charity selection. When you choose a charity to support, your selection is stored on your device and associated with an anonymous identifier on our servers so we can track aggregate donations to each charity.
  • In-app purchases. When you make a purchase, Apple (or Google Play) processes the transaction. We receive only a transaction identifier, the country of purchase, and confirmation of what was purchased. We never receive your name, email, address, or payment card details.

Information collected automatically

  • Gameplay data: scores, plays per day, level progression, and donation totals — tied to an anonymous device identifier, not to you personally.
  • Basic technical data: device type, operating system version, and crash diagnostics, used to keep the App running smoothly.

What we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Payment card or banking information (handled exclusively by Apple or Google)
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, or other on-device content
  • Browsing history or activity in other apps
  • Advertising identifiers — GiveQuest does not display third-party ads

03How We Use Information

We use the limited information we collect to:

  • Run the GiveQuest experience — track your scores, plays, and chosen charity
  • Process and verify in-app purchases
  • Calculate and disburse donations to the charity you select
  • Display aggregate community impact (e.g., "raised across 14 charities by 3,247 players")
  • Improve performance, fix bugs, and prevent fraud
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

04How Donations Are Disbursed

05Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers or marketers. We share information only with:

  • Apple Inc. and Google LLC — for in-app purchase processing, governed by their respective privacy policies
  • Charity disbursement partner — only if applicable (see Section 04)

06Data Retention

  • Local device data (scores, charity selection) remains on your device until you delete the App
  • Anonymized aggregate data may be retained indefinitely for impact reporting
  • In-app purchase records are retained for up to seven years for tax, audit, and compliance purposes

07Children's Privacy

GiveQuest is rated 4+ on the App Store, which means it contains no objectionable content and is appropriate for all ages. The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we don't collect personal information from anyone, this is doubly true.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has somehow provided personal information through the App, please contact us at chiefcraftstudio@gmail.com and we will promptly delete any such information.

In-app purchases require parental approval through Apple's Family Sharing and Ask to Buy features, or Google's equivalent parental controls. We strongly encourage parents to enable these tools.

08Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under privacy laws like the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including:

  • The right to know what data we hold about you
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to opt out of the sale of your data (we don't sell data, ever)
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights

To exercise these rights, email chiefcraftstudio@gmail.com. Because we don't tie data to your identity, we may need help locating any records — typically your anonymous device identifier from the App's Settings screen.

09Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect the limited information we collect. Anonymized data is stored on secured cloud infrastructure with encryption in transit and at rest. No system is perfectly secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection.
