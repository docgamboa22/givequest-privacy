How does the 10% donation model work?

When you make an in-app purchase in GiveQuest — like a Double Down pack — 10% of that purchase is set aside for the charity you've selected. Your selected charity is shown on the Charity tab. You can change it at any time, but only future purchases are affected.

Apple keeps its standard developer fee on the transaction. The remaining 10% donation comes from the developer's net proceeds, not the gross sale price.

Why doesn't GiveQuest require an account or login?

We deliberately built GiveQuest without accounts. No name, no email, no password. The less personal data we collect, the less there is to ever lose, leak, or sell. Your scores, charity selection, and impact totals are tied to an anonymous device identifier — not to you personally.

The trade-off: progress is currently tied to your device. If you delete the app or switch phones, your stats start fresh. We're considering optional cloud sync in a future update.

How do I get a refund on an in-app purchase?

Refunds for in-app purchases are handled by Apple, not by us. To request one:

  • Visit reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Sign in with your Apple ID
  • Find the GiveQuest purchase and tap Report a Problem
  • Choose a reason and submit

If you've already donated through GiveQuest and Apple grants the refund, the corresponding donation may be reversed. Email us if you have questions about a specific case.

Is GiveQuest safe for kids?

Yes. GiveQuest is rated 4+ on the App Store, contains no objectionable content, has no third-party ads, no chat, and no social features. We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Because the app contains in-app purchases, we strongly recommend that parents enable Apple's Family Sharing and Ask to Buy features so that any purchase requires parental approval.

I don't see my favorite charity. Can you add it?

Possibly! We curate the GiveQuest charity list to focus on registered nonprofits with verified disbursement processes. If there's a cause you'd like to see added, email us with the charity name and EIN (if you know it). We can't promise every request, but we read all of them.

The app crashed or is acting weird. What now?

Try these in order:

  • Force-close the app and reopen it
  • Make sure you're running the latest version from the App Store
  • Restart your device
  • If the issue persists, email us with your device model and iOS version. The more detail, the faster we can fix it.
How do I delete my data?

Because GiveQuest doesn't tie data to your identity, deleting the app from your device removes all local data instantly. Anonymized aggregate data (which doesn't identify you) may remain in our impact totals.

If you want any further data review or deletion, email us at chiefcraftstudio@gmail.com. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
